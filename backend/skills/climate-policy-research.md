# Skill: Climate and Environmental Policy Research

## Purpose
Research questions about climate change, air pollution, waste, transport emissions, energy, ecosystems and environmental policy in India, with Goa-specific evidence when relevant.

## Workflow
1. Translate the question into a focused policy question and preserve the requested jurisdiction.
2. Search for official policy documents and implementation guidance, plus independent research and policy analysis.
3. Read accessible source pages; if only a search snippet is available, label it as snippet-only.
4. Extract only statements actually supported by retrieved text. Keep source title, URL, publisher, date if available, and passage.
5. Compare measures across sources. Distinguish an existing policy from a proposed action and an evaluation of effectiveness.
6. Group similar policy actions and report how many distinct sources support each group.
7. Identify evidence gaps, conflicting findings, jurisdiction mismatch and missing implementation details.
8. Never claim that a policy works solely because a source mentions it. Avoid inventing costs, emissions reductions, dates or legal obligations.

## Output requirements
- Policy/action passage
- Supporting source citations
- Support level: multiple sources, single full-page source, or snippet-only
- Responsible stakeholder only when explicitly grounded or clearly labelled as a suggested actor
- Limitations and next evidence to collect

## India / Goa guidance
Prefer current central and state government documents for legal status and implementation. Use CPCB and State Pollution Control Board materials for air-pollution context; use Goa government sources for state-specific claims. Research and NGO reports can add analysis but should not be presented as law.
