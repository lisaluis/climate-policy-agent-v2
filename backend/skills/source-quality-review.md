# Skill: Source Quality Review

## Checklist
- Identify the publisher and the document's actual author.
- Prefer primary government documents for current rules, notifications and schemes.
- Use peer-reviewed research for methods and empirical findings; inspect population, study design and publication date.
- Treat think-tank, NGO, industry and advocacy sources as analysis with their own perspective.
- Do not assume a `.gov` domain guarantees that every claim in a page is current or relevant.
- Do not assume a high citation count means a study is current or directly applicable to India or Goa.
- Record date and jurisdiction; if unavailable, explicitly say so.
- Keep source excerpts short and link to the full source.
- Flag snippet-only evidence and pages that could not be fetched.
